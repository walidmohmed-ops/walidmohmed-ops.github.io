Under the hood

This demo is the condensed layer of a MEL data-systems delivery agent (skill architecture) I built for donor-funded programme work. The full version runs the complete MEL data lifecycle in seven phases with a defined artifact per phase:

0 Intake→ 1 Results architecture QA→ 2 Indicator system→ 3 Collection design→ 4 Pipeline & DQA→ 5 Analysis→ 6 Reporting & learning

Four gates run on every deliverable: data protection & consent · do-no-harm & inclusion · donor compliance · evidence integrity. Core principles: never invent data, full indicator anatomy, attribution honesty, reproducible pipelines — and AI output is a draft for human verification, by design.
