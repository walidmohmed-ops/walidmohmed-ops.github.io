02. Where I've worked

Independent Consultant · Stuttgart region

December 2024 — Present

  • Rural agro-industry across West and North Africa needs process heat, power and fertiliser at once, but grid extension doesn't pay at that density and imported ammonia is priced out of reach. I developed the concept for BIO-Grid Africa — an AI-controlled bio-solar-hydrogen microgrid producing green ammonia on site — and led the European–African research consortium behind it, building a modular proposal library that can be re-cut for Horizon Europe or German federal hydrogen calls without rewriting the technical core.
  • Sudan's conflict displaced millions while employers in the states still functioning cannot fill skilled roles — and returnees arrive with real trade skills that nobody will certify. I designed BRIDGE-Sudan as an employer-linked TVET pilot across two states for a cohort of 100–150: recognition of prior learning so existing skill counts toward a qualification, and placement matched only to employers who have named an actual role and a hiring window, not to letters of interest.
